Working SunD Wallet prototype: screens in Solution 1 and Appendix A3

Why SunD deserves to win

SunD turns Sime Darby's new CREAM rooftops into a closed solar loop that benefits everyone. Owners earn income on every kWh sold and choose credits (+20%) or a bill rebate. The public charges cheaper, with Saver Credits and surplus discounts that turn EV interest into EV ownership. Credits also unlock SunD eBike, Gym and Clubhouse, so demand is built in. Bays run on their own sun, inside and beyond townships. It is feasible on SD's own sites, de-risked by pre-sold credits and modular canopies, and already a working prototype.
