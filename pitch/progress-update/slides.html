Transmission cost · 1/2

Solar is worth more used where it’s made

We compared the same 1 kWh of solar, used two ways

A · Use it on sitePanels feed the building’s own meter (Solar ATAP / SELCO). Each kWh saves the building’s full TNB rate.
B · Send it through TNB’s lines (CREAM)Power goes to a buyer ≤ 5 km away. The buyer saves only the energy part of the bill, and the seller pays a 9 sen toll per kWh.

Why not NFT, tokens or an investment product?

The lawA tradeable token is a security: SC approval, RM 2,000 cap per buyer Securities Commission. Selling for a return is an interest scheme: register, or up to 10 yrs jail / RM 50M Interest Schemes Act
The moneyTokens lose RM 0.59M (option 3). The same roofs sold as an investment earn RM 0.14–0.30M vs RM 1.20M as prepaid credits model
The buyersToken buyers wait 9 yrs to get their money back model; a 6% cash return only matches EPF (~6%)

Business model · 2/2

Chosen: SDP builds the bays, prepaid buyers fund the solar

Option 5 (p9) · 2 SunD Bays + 714 kWp roof solar (+ canopy expansion in year 2) · 25 yrs · before tax model

NPV to SDPRM 2.26Mvalue today of 25 yrs of cash, at 8% a year
IRR12%yearly return on the money put in

SDP cash at the startRM 3.13Mof the RM 6.02M build (p6)

Why this one

  1. Use power where it’s made · no 9 sen toll (p3)
  2. Pay people in credits, not cash · RM 1 of credit costs SDP about RM 0.65 modelmost credits are spent on SDP’s own services; e.g. RM 1 of charging only costs SDP the electricity behind it
  3. Sell a discount, not an investment · no scheme to register (p9)

If nobody prepays, SDP pays for the solar itself (option 1): NPV still RM 1.52M.
